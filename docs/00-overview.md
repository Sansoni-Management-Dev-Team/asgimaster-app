# Product overview

Sansoni's member app gives members a coherent place to understand their membership, set their direction, learn from included content and contact support. It serves iOS and Android members; authorised staff use a separate browser admin for member operations.

The product must show honest identity, access and activity, preserve member work across sessions, and remain useful when upstream systems are unavailable. Membership and billing changes are not implied by an enquiry or a prototype interaction. No public self-registration, in-app purchasing or in-app staff operations are included.

Start with the [feature specifications](features/README.md) for behaviour, states, acceptance and unresolved product choices, then the [high-level designs](hldd/README.md) for selected architecture. [Architecture](architecture.md) shows system boundaries. These documents describe the intended product, not completion or release readiness.

Product success measures and any additional audience definitions require explicit product agreement; they must not be inferred from prototype fixtures or technical telemetry.
